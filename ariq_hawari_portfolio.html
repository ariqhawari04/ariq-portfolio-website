I started out as a mechanical engineer — someone trained to see the world as systems of forces, variables, and constraints. That mindset never left me. It just found a new home in data.

At Telkom Indonesia, I quickly realized that data wasn't just a reporting tool — it was a strategic asset that most teams weren't fully utilizing. I built RFM segmentation models for 10,000+ users not because it was assigned to me, but because I saw an opportunity that hadn't been acted on yet.

"Numbers don't lie, but they don't tell the whole story on their own. My job is to ask the right questions first."

At Mitratel, I moved upstream — closer to the decisions being made in boardrooms. That's where I developed a sharp appreciation for how data needs to be translated differently depending on your audience: an exec wants clarity, a marketer wants levers to pull.

My MSc at Manchester let me go deeper technically. My dissertation tackled a real tension in the Premier League — rising ticket prices alienating fans — by building a multi-objective optimization model that mathematically balanced revenue against fan affordability. It's the kind of problem I love: messy, human, and genuinely important.

Work

Selected projects

Dissertation 2024–2025
AI-driven dynamic ticket pricing for the EPL
Multi-objective optimization · Predictive modelling · Pareto frontier analysis
The problem

Premier League clubs face a real tension — maximize revenue while keeping tickets affordable for loyal fans. No existing framework solved both simultaneously.

What I built

A three-stage pipeline: demand forecasting (RMSE-optimized), multi-objective optimization with fan-fairness constraints, and Pareto frontier mapping for the Big Six clubs.

The outcome

Delivered a tool that empowers club executives to select a pricing policy that balances commercial growth with fanbase accessibility — not a guess, a math-backed decision.

Corporate 2023–2024
Executive KPI & GA4 funneling dashboards
SQL · Metabase · Superset · Looker Studio · Google Analytics 4
The problem

Two teams, two completely different needs — executives needed a high-level operational view; marketers needed granular user journey tracking. One dashboard couldn't serve both.

What I built

End-to-end automated dashboards pulling from internal SQL databases and GA4, built separately for each audience in Metabase and Superset.

The outcome

Significantly faster decision-making for leadership. Marketing team gained visibility into funnel drop-offs they'd never been able to pinpoint before.

Corporate 2022–2023
RFM customer segmentation — Telkom MyIndibiz
SQL · Python · EDA · Customer profiling · Strategic reporting
The problem

The MyIndibiz team was running one-size-fits-all campaigns on 10,000+ users. Without segmentation, budget was being wasted on the wrong customers.

What I built

A custom RFM model tailored to Telkom's subscription logic — not a generic template, but one that accounted for how B2B customers actually buy.

The outcome

Delivered a Proof of Concept with full analytical deck — segmenting users into hibernating, loyal, at-risk, and potential loyalist cohorts to drive targeted campaigns.

Analytics 2022
A/B testing & promotion strategy optimization
EDA · T-test · Hypothesis testing · Statistical analysis
The problem

A fast-food chain ran three different promotional campaigns across randomized locations — but had no rigorous way to determine which actually drove sales.

What I built

A full hypothesis testing framework — EDA to surface trends, T-tests to validate significance, and regression analysis to identify the true drivers.

The outcome

Statistically proved Promotions 1 and 3 significantly outperformed Promotion 2. Disproved the assumption that store age affected sales — saving future budget from a false hypothesis.
